Introduction to Forex

Understand what Forex is, why the market exists, and how currencies are traded around the world.

What is Forex?

Forex, short for foreign exchange, is the global marketplace where currencies are exchanged. It is commonly referred to as the Forex or FX market.

Unlike a traditional stock exchange, Forex does not operate from one central location. Currency trading takes place electronically through a worldwide network of banks, financial institutions, businesses and individual traders.

Key Idea

Forex trading involves exchanging one currency for another. Every Forex trade therefore involves two currencies.

Why Do People Trade Currencies?

Currencies are exchanged for many reasons, including international business, tourism, investment and speculation on changes in exchange rates.

Traders may attempt to benefit from changes in the relative value of one currency compared with another. However, trading involves risk and losses are possible.

The Global Forex Market

The Forex market operates across major financial centres around the world. Because trading moves between different time zones, the market is generally available during the business week, although exact trading hours depend on the market and broker.

Important

Forex trading carries financial risk. Learning the fundamentals and understanding risk management should come before risking real money.
